Some times internet connection gets lost so you just have to wait starring at the screen. Google had a great idea to cheer you up a little. When you see the “Unable to connect to the Internet” screen and the little dinosaur appears, by pressing **space button** dino will start running, and what you have to do is escape the trees. Every time you press space dino will jump and you will go for the high score. Although some times while you play the page will load and you may lose your high-score.
