Recently I got a notification from Facebook so I would get notifications on by browser. Cool I said, let’s do it. But in 10 minutes I was already tired of the notifications, so I wanted to disable them. At first I thought it was some kind of extension so I searched in the extensions page on Chrome but there was nothing.  
The actual place of this option was on my Facebook settings page.  
1. Click the small drop arrow on the top right corner.           ![How to disable Facebook Popup Notifications(1)](/assets/uploads/2015/12/How-to-disable-Facebook-Popup-Notifications1.png)

2. Go to **Settings**.  
![How to disable Facebook Popup Notifications(2)](/assets/uploads/2015/12/How-to-disable-Facebook-Popup-Notifications2.png)

3. Choose **Notifications** and there there is an option called **Desktop and Mobile**.  
![How to disable Facebook Popup Notifications(4)](/assets/uploads/2015/12/How-to-disable-Facebook-Popup-Notifications4.png)

4. From there just Turn Off the Chrome notifications.  
![How to disable Facebook Popup Notifications(5)](/assets/uploads/2015/12/How-to-disable-Facebook-Popup-Notifications5.png)  
[![and its gone](/assets/uploads/2015/12/and-its-gone.png)](/assets/uploads/2015/12/and-its-gone.png)
