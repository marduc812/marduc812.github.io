There is a moment in your life that you were singing *I’m up all night to get lucky* or  *Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger*. The time is now you can play Harder Better Faster with your keyboard! Developer [*Malik Dellidj*](https://twitter.com/Dathink) did that easy for you! He did something simple but really brilliant by creating a virtual keyboard with stored sounds. You click normally on your keyboard and boom!

The best thing is that the project is only for free and open source since you can see the HTML, CSS and Javascript he used.

Have FUN!
