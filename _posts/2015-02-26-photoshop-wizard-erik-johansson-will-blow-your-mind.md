Erik Johansson is a photographer, a *bend reality master* and a wizard of photoshop. He is from Sweden and his art will blow your mind. He works in such a great way that you won’t be able to figure out what happened. He uses so many layers that you just can believe how it’s done.

> He doesn’t capture moments, he captures ideas.

Here are some of the great projects you can find on his [website](http://erikjohanssonphoto.com/).
