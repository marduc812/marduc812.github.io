Theme feature is back again in nightly builds of CM12. And we can find some pretty good Free and Paid themes. Here are some suggestions of what we have till now.

Most of the themes I choose are following the material design. There are a lot of themes based on CM11 but updated to support and CM12.  At the moment I use **Hurricane** which is a really great theme. Collection get’s updated as long as I find a theme that I think is worth sharing.
