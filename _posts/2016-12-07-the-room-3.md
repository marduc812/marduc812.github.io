One of the best puzzle games is now on Google Play for less than 1$. For those who don’t know the game, it has to do with riddles and secrets switches, that turn a simple box into a whole new world. Game is published by Fireproof Games, and has been reviewed by VICE as [probably the Best Mobile Game of 2015](http://www.vice.com/read/why-the-uncommonly-creepy-the-room-3-is-one-of-the-best-mobile-games-of-2015-117).

In this version of the game you have to find clues about a mysterious murder, by solving the riddles on different rooms, traveling and exploring new worlds.  Don’t lose time, this is a great buy for less than 1$.

Download **The Room Three** [[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.FireproofStudios.TheRoom3&hl=en)] [[App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/the-room-three/id918054748?mt=8)]
